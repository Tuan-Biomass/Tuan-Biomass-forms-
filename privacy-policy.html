Privacy & Data Collection Policy

Effective 17 August 2026 · Covers all induction, sign-in and check-in forms on this site

What we collect

Through our induction, sign-in and truck check-in forms we collect your name, company, contact details, licence and induction records, and uploaded documents such as insurance certificates. Truck driver check-in also records your PIN and vehicle details.

Why we collect it

To confirm you're inducted and authorised before entering site, keep accurate records for emergency muster, and meet our WHS and Chain of Responsibility obligations.

How it's stored

Submissions are sent securely and stored within Tuan Biomass / Albioma's Microsoft 365 environment (SharePoint/Excel). Access is limited to staff who need it for site safety and access administration.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. It's only shared with authorised Tuan Biomass / Albioma staff, our IT service providers who help run this system, and regulators where required by law.

You can ask us what we hold about you, request a correction, or raise a concern — see contact details below. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the OAIC.
