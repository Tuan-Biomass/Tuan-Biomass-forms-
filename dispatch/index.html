On Arrival

Truck Check-In

All drivers must check in each time they visit the site. The Truck Check-In system records who is attending site and checks your current induction and compliance information.

When you arrive
  • 1Scan the QR code on the Truck Check-In sign using your phone.
  • 2Enter your check-in code, which is the last 4 digits of your phone number.
  • 3Follow the instructions displayed on your phone.
  • 4Complete the check-in before continuing onto site.
Your phone number links your check-in to your existing driver record. The QR code and check-in instructions are displayed on the Truck Check-In sign.
Every visit: You must complete a new check-in for every site visit.
Legal Obligations

Chain of Responsibility (CoR)

Under CoR laws, everyone in the transport supply chain shares responsibility for ensuring heavy vehicle safety and compliance with the Heavy Vehicle National Law (HVNL).

  • 1Ensure accurate load documentation and adherence to weight limits to prevent overloading.
  • 2Follow safe loading and unloading procedures to minimise risks.
  • 3Report any unsafe practices or breaches of CoR obligations immediately.
  • 4Vehicles must be roadworthy and properly maintained before entering the site.
Fatigue Management

Fatigue Requirements

  • 1Comply with work and rest hour regulations as per the NHVR.
  • 2Maintain an accurate and up-to-date log book.
  • 3Take mandatory rest breaks and not exceed legal driving limits.
  • 4Report any fatigue-related concerns to site management immediately.
  • 5If feeling fatigued, stop work and seek assistance — do not continue operating a vehicle unsafely.

    Loading Procedure

    Dispatch Loading Process

    The following process outlines the steps drivers must follow when collecting a load from site. Follow the sequence from arrival through to departure, including traffic movements, loading, weight checks and securing the load.

    If you are unsure at any stage, stop and contact the loader operator on UHF Channel 34.
    Weight Compliance

    Overweight Vehicle Procedure

    If a vehicle is identified or suspected to be overweight, it must not leave site until the load has been corrected.

    Follow the process shown below to confirm the permitted mass, adjust the load and recheck the vehicle weight. If the vehicle remains overweight, repeat the adjustment process until the vehicle is compliant and safe to leave site.

    Weight Compliance

    Overweight Load Reporting

    If the weighbridge indicates that your vehicle may be overweight, you must report it immediately.

    As soon as you become aware of the overload:
    • 1Call (07) 4123 4734.

    Load Security

    Tarping Requirements

    All pellet loads must be fully covered and secured before leaving site.

    • 1Tarps must be waterproof and in good condition.
    • 2Tarps with holes, damage or that are not waterproof may be refused loading at Tuan.
    • 3The tarp must fully cover the load and have no loose sections.
    • 4Do not climb onto the trailer or load to adjust the tarp.
    • 5If you cannot safely operate or adjust the tarp, ask Tuan staff for assistance using mobile plant.
    • 6Do not leave site until the load is properly covered and secured.
    Emergency Procedures

    In Case of an Emergency

    • 1Stop all work immediately.
    • 2Call for help using site communication channels or notify the nearest worker.
    • 3Follow the emergency evacuation plan and proceed to the nearest assembly point.
    • 4Do not return to work until the all-clear is given by the site supervisor.
    Fire or Hazardous Incident: Report smoke, fire, or a hazardous spill immediately to the site office or on-shift operator. Evacuate to the designated assembly point.
    Medical Emergency: Dial 000. Do not move an injured person unless they are in immediate danger.
