05 Fire Watch & High Risk Standby Personnel
The permit issuer cannot act as the fire watch. A standby person is a separate role, required where hot work is combined with confined space entry, working at heights, or other high-risk activities.
0 – 30 Minutes
Continuous monitoring. Fire watch remains on-site at all times. No breaks permitted during this period.
30 – 60 Minutes
Periodic checks. Every 10–15 minutes. Check for smoke, embers and residual heat.
60 – 90 Minutes
Periodic checks continue. Every 10–15 minutes. Minimum 90 min total from work completion.
In high-risk environments (e.g. biomass dust present, elevated fire load), monitoring periods must be extended at the permit issuer's discretion.
